        Daytona × Nosana · HackSprint Tokyo
      

      
The web scraper that
repairs itself and can see.

      
SandScrape races extraction strategies in isolated sandboxes, keeps the fastest valid one, and reads charts and product photos with a VLM on decentralized GPUs.

    


    
    

      
01 — THE PAIN

      
Scrapers are brittle and blind

      

        
Selectors rot
One class rename or redesign and a CSS/XPath scraper silently returns nothing. Teams spend their time babysitting pipelines.

        
JavaScript hides content
SPAs, lazy-loaded tables and infinite scroll mean the raw HTML is often an empty shell. You don't know which tool a site needs until you try.

        
Images are a blind spot
Charts, infographics and product photos end up as bare <img> URLs. Text-only LLMs and RAG pipelines never see what they show.

Isolation — untrusted pages and headless browsers run in throwaway sandboxes, never on our host.

        
Cost hygiene — losers are cancelled the moment a winner validates; auto-delete is a backstop.

        
Resilience — cold GPU node? A circuit breaker routes to a fallback VLM and labels it honestly.
